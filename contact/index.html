§ 01 / About · Bio關於 · 簡介

About.關於

This site collects notes, tools, and experiments on data science, finance, and operations research. The focus is on clear methods, reproducible analysis, and practical applications. 這裡收錄數據科學、金融與運籌學的筆記、工具和實驗，著重清晰的方法、可重現的分析，以及實際應用。

Browse the projects for working examples, or the insights for write-ups on modeling, optimization, and software tools. 可以在專案查看實作範例，或到觀察閱讀建模、優化與軟件工具的文章。

Some project descriptions are generalized. Source data is omitted when it is not public. 部分專案介紹經過概括處理；非公開數據不會在本站展示。
